# Pull-forward PO screening: narrow follow-up

1. Use flagged materials, destination and first risk week from current request or prior-read shortlist; default Scenario 1 where the PO module uses scenarios. Discover only relevant PO line modules and fields: exact material identity, receiving location, PO number/line, remaining open quantity, commitment/status, expected receipt date and modeled week. Read bounded filtered lines completely; do not refresh donor locations or whole inventory forecast unless asked for a new complete analysis.
2. Identify lines with verified open + committed status and material identity and due **after** first at-risk week. Show potential covered quantity, capped by remaining open line quantity; do not sum recurring unmet-demand weeks or assume partial delivery is possible. If due before the risk but shortage persists, investigate availability/allocation instead of suggesting an expedite. If none verified, say 'none identified in checked lines'.
3. Compare expected receipt dates to actual read date: past-dated 'open' POs are stale/unverified, not a present deliverable commitment. Verify contradictory displayed descriptions and ask procurement to confirm live balance, accepted new arrival date and lead time. Name material, provisional quantity, destination and target week on each action; rank **checks**, not unproven feasible outcomes. Do not change POs.
